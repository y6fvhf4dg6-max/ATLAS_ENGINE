crop boundary to a small 24-edge neck aperture.

- original head: 13,422 vertices / 26,692 faces;
- harmonic result: 13,806 vertices / 27,606 faces;

- original head vertex maximum change: 0.0 mm;

Corrected Blender rendering revealed:

- a wide lower-head shelf;
- fan-like stretched surface triangles;
- non-anatomical convergence toward the small neck aperture;
- visual failure despite the technically controlled boundary count.

Decision:

`CURRENT_SUBJECT_SPECIFIC_HARMONIC_SHELF = REJECTED_AS_GENERAL_SOLUTION`

Do not continue the mainline by cleaning, eroding or smoothing that shelf.

Rejected/repeated routes include:

- trim-only aggressive face removal;
- full 170-loop direct retopology;
- radial/fan convergence;
- shelf erosion;
- global remesh;
- voxel repair;
- uncontrolled boolean repair;
- smoothing that changes approved identity geometry.

The technical experiment remains historical evidence.

# 9. REQUIRED GENERAL SOLUTION

The product family requires:

`ADAPTIVE_HEAD_BODY_ASSEMBLY`

For each approved head/body pair ATLAS must:

1. preserve the approved face, hair and head identity;
2. find a safe lower-head attachment region;
3. estimate the natural neck center, direction and scale;
4. inspect the target body neck interface;
5. align the body to the approved head where possible;
6. build a short, bounded local transition;
7. avoid visible identity-geometry modification;
8. reject shelf, fan, funnel and stretched-triangle artifacts;
9. verify topology and visual continuity;
10. preserve rollback to the approved source head.

One subject's fixed neck coordinate is not a universal product contract.
