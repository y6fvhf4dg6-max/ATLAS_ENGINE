# 1. Purpose

P-3 shall compare commercially admissible photo-to-3D generation routes and select a primary and fallback route from verified identity, geometry, cost, latency, privacy, licensing and revision evidence.

An attractive result that does not recognizably represent the approved subject is a failure.

`IDENTITY_BEFORE_STYLE`

# 2. Evidence boundary

- No real customer photograph may be uploaded during this benchmark.
- Use only synthetic evidence, provider demonstrations, or pilot evidence whose processing authority is already recorded.
- Every result must identify provider, plan, model/version, job ID, input class, date, settings and output hash.
- Vendor statements must be labelled separately from observed ATLAS evidence.
- One successful Meshy V7 pilot is subject-specific evidence, not general pipeline proof.
- No provider becomes approved solely because commercial output use is advertised.

# 5. Benchmark input protocol

Every compared route shall receive materially equivalent authorized evidence:

- the same approved frontal reference;
- the same left and right three-quarter references where supported;
- the same profile and hair references where supported;
- the same neutral identity instruction;
- the same requested output class;
- no provider-specific identity-enhancing manual edit before initial comparison;
- all provider limitations and unsupported views recorded.

Single-image and multi-image routes must be reported as separate benchmark classes.

# 6. Required benchmark record

| Group | Required evidence | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Standardized neutral renders; human likeness judgment; preserved critical anchors | Human identity rejection overrides aggregate score |
| Geometry | Components, triangles, watertightness, winding, self-intersection evidence, usable head/hair surface | Repairable defects recorded separately from identity failure |
| Integration | Lower-head completeness, neck/interface availability, safe crop/attachment region | Does not claim adaptive assembly proof |
| Appearance | Texture/material availability, hair and accessory fidelity, neutral color reference | Appearance cannot override identity failure |
| Repeatability | At least three runs per route/input class where credits permit | One attractive output is insufficient |
| Latency | Queue, generation, download and operator-review time | Report median and range |
| Cost | Subscription, credits, failed generations, artist correction and regeneration burden | Report effective accepted-candidate cost |
| Privacy | Training use, public/private state, retention, deletion, subprocessors and transfer safeguards | Mandatory gate; unresolved means customer upload blocked |
| License | Input authority, output commercial rights, redistribution/manufacture rights and plan restrictions | Mandatory gate; unresolved means commercial route blocked |
| Operations | API availability, job ID, export formats, automation and failure recovery | Manual steps and provider lock-in remain explicit |

# 7. Classification and scoring

Each factual field shall be labelled:

- `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL_FACT`;
- `OBSERVED_ATLAS_EVIDENCE`;
- `VENDOR_CLAIM_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_PROVEN`;
- `INFERENCE`;
- `UNRESOLVED`.

Scored comparison uses 100 points only after the privacy and license gates pass:

- identity retention: 35;
- head/hair completeness and production geometry: 20;
- repeatability: 10;
- correction burden: 10;
- latency: 5;
- effective cost: 10;
- API/operational control: 10.

Privacy and commercial license are mandatory eligibility gates and are not compensable score items.

# 8. Stop and rejection rules

A route is rejected or held when any applies:

1. recognizable identity fails human judgment;
2. critical identity anchors are systematically replaced by a generic face;
3. customer inputs may be public or used for training without an approved contractual basis;
4. commercial manufacture/output rights are absent or unresolved;
5. deletion, retention or international-transfer conditions cannot meet the approved customer-data contract;
6. output cannot support an identity-safe head preparation route;
7. accepted-result cost or human correction burden defeats the target product economics;
8. the provider cannot supply reproducible job/version provenance.

# 9. P-3 evidence sequence

1. Complete written legal/privacy fact sheets for Rodin, Meshy and Tripo.
2. Lock an authorized non-customer benchmark evidence set.
3. Produce equivalent initial candidates.
4. Conduct blind human identity review before topology repair.
5. Measure geometry and production-preparation burden only for identity-surviving candidates.
6. Record latency, credits, retries and operator time.
7. Select primary and fallback routes.
8. Present GATE-P3 decision for user approval.
