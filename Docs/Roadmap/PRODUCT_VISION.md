# ATLAS PRODUCT VISION
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-07-06

---

# ATLAS Philosophy

ATLAS is not an STL generator.

ATLAS is a semantic city modeling engine that automatically converts any location in the world into premium-quality, production-ready 3D printable city models.

Every architectural decision must support this vision.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Become one of the world's highest-quality automatic city model generation engines.

Success is measured by:

- visual quality
- print quality
- automation
- production cost
- scalability

—not by STL triangle count.

---

# PRODUCT GOALS

## GOAL 1 — READABILITY

A customer must immediately recognize:

- roads
- neighborhoods
- parks
- rivers
- landmarks
- city structure

within the first few seconds.

The road network is the backbone of the product.

---

## GOAL 2 — PREMIUM VISUAL QUALITY

Every exported model should look like a premium display object.

Characteristics:

- clean geometry
- smooth roads
- readable city layout
- balanced proportions
- elegant shadows
- professional appearance

---

## GOAL 3 — FDM FIRST DESIGN

Models must be designed specifically for FDM printers.

Priorities:

- support-free
- reliable printing
- minimum print failures
- optimized filament usage
- optimized print time

The engine should never generate geometry that unnecessarily increases production cost.

---

## GOAL 4 — SEMANTIC CITY ENGINE

ATLAS must understand the city.

Not just polygons.

The engine should identify:

- buildings
- roads
- rivers
- parks
- bridges
- railways
- squares
- landmarks
- terrain

Every object has its own modeling rules.

---

## GOAL 5 — PARAMETRIC ENGINE

Every important value must be configurable.

Examples:

- road width
- road depth
- building height
- water level
- park level
- frame width
- frame height
- terrain resolution
- print resolution

Changing a parameter must not require rewriting algorithms.

---

## GOAL 6 — AUTOMATIC PRODUCTION

Customer workflow:

Location

↓

Preview

↓

Automatic Scene Generation

↓

Automatic STL Generation

↓

Automatic Production

↓

Shipment

Human intervention should be minimized.

---

## GOAL 7 — SCALABILITY

The same engine must generate:

- Frankfurt
- Paris
- Tokyo
- Istanbul
- New York
- small villages
- historical centers

without project-specific code.

---

## GOAL 8 — LAYER SYSTEM

Every exported scene should follow the same hierarchy.

Frame

↓

Base Plate

↓

Terrain

↓

Roads

↓

Parks

↓

Water

↓

Buildings

↓

Landmarks

Future layers should integrate naturally into this architecture.

---

## GOAL 9 — PRODUCT PLATFORM

ATLAS is not a single product.

It is a platform.

Future products include:

- City Models
- My Life Map
- Our Love Map
- Family Heritage
- Personalized Relief Models
- Historical Collections
- Limited Editions

All products should share the same engine whenever possible.

---

# COMPETITIVE STRATEGY

The market is highly competitive.

ATLAS will NOT compete only by price.

ATLAS will compete through:

- higher automation
- lower production cost
- cleaner geometry
- better readability
- faster generation
- premium appearance

Automation creates cost advantage.

Quality creates brand value.

Both are mandatory.

---

# ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

When making technical decisions:

Do not ask:

"Does this code work?"

Instead ask:

"Does this move ATLAS closer to becoming the world's best automatic city modeling engine?"

If the answer is no,

the implementation should be reconsidered.

---

# LONG TERM VISION

ATLAS should eventually become the reference platform for personalized 3D geographic products worldwide.

Every meaningful place can become a physical object.

Every memory has a location.

ATLAS transforms locations into lasting objects.
